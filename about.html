ℹ️ About the Project

EduSpark – A Digital Learning Space

Our Project

EduSpark is a student-focused digital learning project designed to make Class 10 learning more engaging and accessible. It brings together lesson topics, examples, practice activities and helpful learning resources in one simple interface. The goal is to encourage curiosity, confidence and independent learning.

Project Objectives

  • Make learning resources easy to explore.
  • Connect textbook concepts with practical examples.
  • Encourage self-assessment through quizzes.
  • Support teachers with simple classroom ideas.
