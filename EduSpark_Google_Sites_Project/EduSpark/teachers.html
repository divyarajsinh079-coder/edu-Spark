👩‍🏫 Teacher's Corner

Helpful ideas for planning lessons and supporting student learning.

📘 Lesson Planning

Set a clear learning objective, introduce the topic with a relatable example, and finish with a short recap.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Classroom Activities

Encourage group discussion, peer learning, demonstrations and short knowledge checks.

⭐ Teaching Tip

Connect each new idea to everyday life. Ask students to explain the concept in their own words.
