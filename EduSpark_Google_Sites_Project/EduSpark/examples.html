💡 The Example Skill

Understand classroom concepts by connecting them with everyday life.

🌱 Science: Plant Growth

A plant needs sunlight, water, air and nutrients to grow. Observe a plant at home and record how it changes over a week.

📐 Mathematics: Geometry Around Us

Identify triangles, rectangles and circles in windows, road signs, books and household objects.

🌍 Environment: Reduce Waste

Separate wet and dry waste at home. This practical habit demonstrates responsible resource management.
